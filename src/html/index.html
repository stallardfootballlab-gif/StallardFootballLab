03 — The Training Methodology

Built on detail. Proven in matches.

01

Global Technical Development

We pride ourselves on honing in on the fine details that matter when it comes to being the best footballer possible. Our focused performance drills fine-tune the fundamentals that set players apart, including body shape, scanning, clean footwork in tight spaces, explosive speed, passing detail, ball striking and much more.

Body shape Scanning Footwork Ball striking
02

Real-Match Simulation & Integration

Cones don't tackle. We take technical skills out of isolation and force players to apply them in high-pressure scenarios. By learning to make split-second decisions and adapting to real defensive pressure, players smoothly transition their work in training onto the pitch at the weekend.

05 — FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Send a message and Ben will reply personally.

How long does a 1-2-1 session last?

1-2-1 sessions run for either 45 or 60 minutes, depending on the player's age, fitness and goals. We'll recommend the best length during your first conversation.

What equipment does my child need?

Football boots suited to the surface, shin pads, comfortable training kit and a water bottle. All balls, cones and training equipment are provided.

Do you train beginners?

Yes. Sessions are built around each player, from young beginners building confidence to grassroots players preparing for academy trials.

How do I book the first session?

Send a message through the contact form below or via WhatsApp. We'll arrange a time, location and focus for the first session.

Can you provide a tailored training plan for my daughter to develop her skills?

Absolutely. Every player receives coaching tailored to their position, strengths and goals, and the SFL Performance programme includes a full individual development plan.

Can you analyse my son in a match?

Yes. Video feedback and match analysis are part of the SFL Performance programme, linking what happens on matchday directly to the work we do in training.
